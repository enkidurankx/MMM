DENKI 電気

An instrument for the sounds of FuMi-1: 21 voices on a 6-operator FM engine (koto, harp, chimes, rin, shakuhachi, flutes, sho, kokyu, strings, taiko, wood blocks), played from a chromatic keyboard with polyphonic aftertouch.

Playing

  • Strike: the lower you hit a key, the harder the note.
  • Aftertouch (per note): keep the finger down and slide it up the key. Pressure swells the note, gives it its own vibrato and a little edge. A pen or a pressure-sensing screen adds its own pressure.
  • Bend (per note): slide sideways, up to two semitones.
  • MIDI: poly aftertouch, channel pressure and MPE (pitch bend and pressure per channel) all work.
  • Computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P, Z / X octave, Shift = full pressure on the held notes.
  • Hands: the koto's left hand and the other techniques of FuMi-1, held while you press the pad.
