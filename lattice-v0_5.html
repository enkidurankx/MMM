lattice

A cellular resonator space: sixteen resonators on a grid, tuned like the modes of a plate, a drum or a bell, or to a scale of your choice. Each one half sings by itself and hears its neighbours. They pull each other into step, tire, and take over from one another, so the sound changes slowly on its own. Touch the grid to strike or rub it. Silent until you start it.
