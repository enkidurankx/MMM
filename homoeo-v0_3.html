homoeo

An autonomous feedback system: a saturating fold, three band-pass filters with a delay each, intermodulation and a self-regulating gain in a closed loop, started by a noise floor, a burst or the microphone, and then left to find its own balance. Silent until you start it. The microphone only listens while you hold its button. Use headphones: with speakers the room closes a second, uncontrolled loop.

1Input source of the loop▾

Hold the button (or the M key) to let the microphone in; let go and it is silent again. The first press asks the browser for permission. Captured raw: no echo cancellation, noise suppression or auto gain.

2Homeostasis self-regulating gain

The bar shows how far the bands hold themselves back right now. Each band: gain = 1 / (1 + damping x its level), so the louder a band gets, the harder it pulls back. Small-signal loop gain = Feedback x Drive x Fold factor: below 1 the system dies out, above 1 it keeps sounding.

3Nonlinearity fold and intermodulation

Drive saturates (tanh), Fold bends the result through a sine. Intermod blends the plain sum of the three bands (0 %) with the product of two bands (100 %): new sum and difference tones. Coupling is how much the bands hear each other: 100 % = one shared loop (the spec: usually one band wins and the others fade), 0 % = three separate loops that do not hear each other.

4Filter bank 3 band-pass filters in parallel

LFO 2 → filter base frequency  

Depth 100 % = base frequency x0.35 ... x2.8. Spacing is the distance between the bands in octaves (low, middle, high band).

5Delays one per band

At x1.00 the three delays are 47, 79 and 131 ms. They share no common period, so the echoes keep interfering.

LFO 1 → delay shift  

Depth 100 % = all three delay times x0.5 ... x2.
