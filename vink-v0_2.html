VINK·LOOP

A recursive feedback network after Jaap Vink: a ring modulator, filters, tape saturation and a delay in a closed loop, fed by a microphone or just by its own noise. Silent until you start it. The microphone only listens while you hold its button. Use headphones: with speakers the room closes a second, uncontrolled loop.

VINK·LOOP

1Input source of the loop

Hold the button (or the M key) to let the microphone in; let go and it is silent again. The first press asks the browser for permission. Captured raw: no echo cancellation, noise suppression or auto gain.

2Loop feedback and tape stage

Feedback near 0.8 and above lets the loop hold; above 1 it saturates in the tape stage instead of running away.

4Filter high-pass, then low-pass
