# lichen (web) tests

`lichen-v0_5.html` is **spectral growth**. A row of up to 128 partials, one per cell of a one-dimensional reaction-diffusion field (Gray-Scott: the substrate U is eaten by a growing V, V spreads into its neighbours, V dies at a rate DECAY, U is fed at a rate GROWTH). Where V is large, the partial of that cell sounds. The row is ordered by frequency (ORDER: harmonic, stretched, plate, bell, primes, continuum), so a patch of V is a patch of neighbouring partials: a formant that grows, splits and dies. Nothing is a sample or a note: no input, no clock. Touch the field to plant a seed.
Same shell as the other feedback apps (see `tests/feedbacks/README.md`); rose base colour.

**What the tests found while building (this is why the app looks the way it does):**
- A Gray-Scott field in one dimension **freezes**. I scanned F 0.014 ... 0.07 and k 0.04 ... 0.075 (64 and 96 cells, four ratios of the two diffusion rates): almost everywhere the pattern is a set of fixed spots after a few thousand steps (movement 0.000) or the field dies. Moving patterns (pulses that split and merge) exist only in a narrow strip (F 0.018 ... 0.022, k 0.046 ... 0.054), and a constant drift along the row (a "wind") sweeps all of them out of the field. This is the same failure as lattice and knot ("it settles"), in the purest form: with SPORES, WEATHER, REGULATE, SHIMMER and JITTER at 0 the minute-long variation of level and tone colour is 0.000.
- What keeps it alive: **spores** (random seeds, and one whenever the field has been dead for 0.25 s), **weather** (F and k walk, 17 s and 23 s), the **partials** (shimmer in level, jitter in pitch) and a **regulator**. The regulator measures how much the field moves (change per cell per second, in units of a 300-steps-per-second field) and whether anything is alive. Frozen and alive: it lowers F a little (towards the edge, where the field moves); dying: it raises F quickly. The result is a slow cycle around the edge between frozen and dead, by design. Measured over 90 s and three seeds: frozen 0 % of the time and dead 0 ... 4 % with REGULATE 70 %, against frozen 100 % without it. The gain of the regulator matters (0.002 per second left the field frozen 30 ... 57 % of the time, 0.005 gives 0 %).
- A real bug the long runs found: a partial that was activated while it was too quiet to be set up had a rotation of 0, which drove its phasor to zero for good; after a minute or two more and more partials were silent although the field was alive (rms exactly 0). Fixed: too quiet means resting, the rotation starts at 1 and a collapsed phasor is restarted.
- Level: partials are summed and then compressed by the total power (the level grows with the growth, but not without end). 128 partials of the continuum order, close together, beat into peaks: the presets are levelled and checked for peaks below 0.9.
- RESET did only fade out and in; the field was not cleared (the clearing was tested for at the wrong moment). Now it clears when the output is silent and plants three seeds.

- `node tests/lichen/dsp.test.js` (about 8 minutes) - runs the AudioWorklet code of the page in Node: starts by itself at 44.1 / 48 / 96 kHz; the order of the partials (harmonic, bell, primes, continuum, stretch, plate, warp, pitch); the line height in the spectrum at each partial follows the amplitude the meter reports (correlation above 0.9 over 8 runs); DENSITY, WIDTH (0 is mono), LEVEL; the field freezes without help and the regulator keeps it moving; a field that cannot live is restarted by a seed; seeds land where they are put; WEATHER and both LFOs; three minutes with the defaults do not settle (and without the helpers the same minute is static); every preset for 150 s (no hole below -55 dB, peak below 0.9, variation of level and colour); worst cases and 20 corner settings stay below 0.97 (the limiter); RESET; CPU (64 partials about 8 %, 128 partials with a fast field about 11 % of one core in Node).
- `PW=/opt/node22/lib/node_modules/playwright SHOTS=/tmp/shots node tests/feedbacks/browser.test.js lichen` - the shared browser test of the series plus the lichen parts: starts by itself, readouts (loudest partial with frequency and note, how the field moves), the field view draws, it has changed after 12 s, touch plants seeds, the Seed button, the two pads, ORDER and the fold of Material, REGULATE and SPORES at 0.
- `node tests/feedbacks/clicks.test.js lichen` - no clicks when a parameter changes while the sound runs. The test sound is a field that has almost stopped (SPEED 10, no shimmer or jitter); the parameters that act on the field move only a little.

**What this does not prove:** how it sounds. Whether a field of growing and dying partials is a world worth listening to or a slowly flickering organ, whether the regulator's cycle around the edge is audible as a pulse (it takes tens of seconds), Safari / iOS, and the CPU on a phone.
