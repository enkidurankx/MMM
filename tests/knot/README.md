# knot (web) tests

`knot-v0_1.html` is a knot of six coupled oscillators. Each one hears the others in three ways: its phase is pulled toward theirs (**LOCK**: they fall into step; a pair snaps to the nearest simple ratio p : q up to 6 : 6, so harmonic sets lock into clean chords), its phase is bent by their waves (**MOD**: FM, sidebands) and its wave is multiplied by theirs (**RING**: AM); **SELF** bends an oscillator by its own last wave. A wave folder (FOLD, SKEW) shapes each wave. RATIOS: unison, harmonics, fifths, square roots; DETUNE nudges each off its ratio; COUNT fades oscillators in and out; LINKS: each hears the one before, both neighbours or all; TWIST: hearing one way more than the other.
What makes it develop: the grip of each link follows how locked its pair is (**ADAPT**): a pair that has fallen into step loses its grip and drifts apart, an unlocked pair is pulled together again; **EVOLVE** is how slowly. **WANDER** lets each pitch walk. **SHAKE** lets all links go for 30 ms and brings them back over 3 s. The view shows the ring of oscillators (the dot is the phase, the line is the link: thickness = grip, brightness = lock) and an XY pad (LOCK across, MOD up). LFO 1 moves MOD, LFO 2 the pitch of the knot (both up to 1 kHz; at audio rate FM).
Same shell as the other feedback apps (see `tests/feedbacks/README.md`); green base colour. Silent until the first click; no input, no microphone.

**What the tests found while building:** the first version pulled phases directly (Kuramoto): with the UNISON set the whole knot locked into one pitch and stayed there (a constant tone), with the harmonic or inharmonic sets nothing locked at all (the coherence is meaningless for oscillators at different pitches). The lock now works at the nearest simple ratio (psi = q phi_i - p phi_j), so a harmonic set can lock into clean intervals. The grip then settled at a fixed value (a stable point) until a second slow lag was put into the loop (the grip follows a slow average of the coherence, not the coherence itself): now there are slow waves of the grip (period 10 ... 20 s) around a mean, but the overall level stays constant, so the change is in the timbre and the beating, not in loudness. Both a faster sine approximation (parabola) and a cheaper cosine were tried for speed: the parabola was slower in V8 than Math.sin. SHAKE first re-randomised the phases (a click); it now lets the links go and returns them (no click).
CPU in Node: the default ring about 21 %, both neighbours 26 %, everyone hears everyone 45 ... 54 % of one core (every link costs a sine per sample), 3 oscillators 13 %.

- `node tests/knot/dsp.test.js` (about 8 minutes) - runs the AudioWorklet code of the page in Node: sounds from the first moment and stays bounded; one oscillator alone is a clean sine at the PITCH (0.1 %, harmonics more than 50 dB down), PITCH doubles; the four ratio sets by FFT; DETUNE; COUNT; MOD, RING and SELF make sidebands, FOLD adds odd and SKEW even overtones; LOCK (two oscillators 4 % apart beat at 0, are in step at 80 %); ADAPT 0 keeps the grip at 1, ADAPT 100 % loosens it by more than 30 % and it keeps moving; WANDER differs run to run (2 ... 3 Hz), 0 is exactly the same; SHAKE without a click; reset; LFOs slow and at audio rate (FM sidebands at +- 40 Hz); 24 corner and random settings at 44.1 / 48 / 96 kHz; everything open and closed again; CPU.
- `PW=/opt/node22/lib/node_modules/playwright SHOTS=/tmp/shots node tests/feedbacks/browser.test.js knot` - the shared browser test of the series plus the knot parts: sounds, the ring view and the pad are drawn, the grip has changed after 15 s, the pad sets LOCK and MOD (and the fader follows), Shake, Reset.
- `node tests/feedbacks/clicks.test.js knot` - no clicks when a parameter changes while the sound runs (see `tests/feedbacks/README.md`); one oscillator with all the coupling off is used as the smooth sound.

**What this does not prove:** how it sounds (whether the slow changes of the grip are audible and pleasant, or just a steady FM drone), Safari / iOS, the CPU load on a phone (everyone-hears-everyone is the expensive one).
