# lattice (web) tests

`lattice-v0_3.html` is the "cellular resonator space": sixteen resonators on a 4 x 4 grid, each tuned to one mode of a plate, a drum, a string or a set of bells (MODES; the set glides when you change it). Each cell is regenerative: part of its own sound goes back into it through a soft clip (REGEN, DRIVE), and above a loop gain of 1 it sings by itself from the hiss. The cells hear their neighbours (COUPLE; LINKS = grid, ring, everyone or a few random links; TWIST = hearing one way more than the other).
Four slow things make it develop on its own: **tiring** (a cell that has been loud for long loses loop gain and rests; REST TIME is how slowly), **rivalry** (a loud cell takes loop gain from its neighbours), **pull** (a loud cell bends up in pitch, like a struck plate) and **drift** (every cell wanders a little in pitch). A touch on the grid strikes the cells near it and rubs while the finger rests; several fingers work together. LFO 1 moves the coupling, LFO 2 the pitch of the whole space (both up to 1 kHz).
**v0.2:** nine ORDERS of the cell pitches (PLATE, DRUM, BELLS with church-bell partials, STRING, JUST, SLENDRO, PENTA, BOHLEN-PIERCE, FIFTHS), STRETCH (spreads the intervals) and WARP/SEED (random detuning, New warp), a bell SHIMMER (a second resonator per cell at an inharmonic PARTIAL ratio), QUALITY LITE / ECO / FULL (cells run at 1/4, 1/2 or full sample rate; LITE with 12 cells is about 10-12 % of a core in Node, FULL with 16 cells about 28 %; the default is LITE with 12 cells), Life & Evolution on top with two XY pads, folding groups for Tuning / Bell partial / Coupling / Scope, and a soft limiter (ceiling 0.95, hard clamp 0.97) so the output never reaches 0 dB. Not heard, only measured.
**Settling (found after v0.2 shipped):** measured over 4 minutes the cells did settle: the spread of the cell energies fell from 0.20 in the first minute to 0.08 and stayed there (the flips counted per second were only chatter around the threshold). One slow lag in the feedback has one stable point. Fix: tiring is now a two-stage lag with a different speed for every cell (so the cells cycle at unrelated rates), plus WANDER, a slow random drift of each cell's own loop gain. The spread is now 0.3 and stays at 0.3 for the whole 4 minutes (test: minute 3 at least 70 % of minute 1). All twelve presets were measured over 3 minutes: the spread stays at 0.15 ... 0.4; Bell tower, Pentatonic fog, Swarm, Tide and Breathing got their own WANDER (and Bell tower, fog and Tide a little more REGEN) because they had only 1 ... 2 cells awake.
Same shell as the other feedback apps (see `tests/feedbacks/README.md`); gold base colour. Silent until the first click; no input, no microphone.

**What the tests found while building:** with tiring alone the cells settled into a steady state (variation of a cell's energy over time 0.01 ... 0.04: nothing happens, the same problem as the no-input mixer I removed). A slow negative feedback on a fast cell has one stable point. What made it live was RIVALRY: cells that take loop gain from their neighbours, plus tiring, make the activity move around (variation 1.1 ... 1.9, three to six cells awake out of sixteen, a cell changes state every few seconds). A long REST TIME is not calmer but more restless (it is a delay in the feedback). The cells are narrow band-passes: a cell answers only near its own pitch, so neighbours at other pitches hardly hear each other and coupling mostly acts through the clip (overtones of one cell land on another, best in the STRING set) and between cells close in pitch; locking two cells 20 Hz apart does not happen. DRIFT was 100 times too small in the first version (the random walk was not scaled). A high Q at a low pitch takes many seconds to grow out of the hiss (110 Hz, Q 300, regen 1.3: about 25 s).

- `node tests/lattice/dsp.test.js` (about 8 minutes) - runs the AudioWorklet code of the page in Node: starts by itself and stays bounded; silent below the threshold without hiss; one cell at the PITCH (2 %), four cells of the STRING set at 1, 2, 3, 4 x, PLATE at 1 x and 2.5 x, BELLS at 1 x and 1.19 x, PITCH doubles; CELLS 8 silences cells 9 ... 16; PULL raises the pitch of a loud cell by 2 % or more; DRIFT makes the pitch differ from run to run; development: variation 0.4 or more with the defaults, below 0.2 without TIRING and RIVALRY, three or more cells awake and never all sixteen, REST TIME changes the pattern; COUPLE changes the pattern; four topologies with TWIST; a strike in the top-left corner wakes the cells there, a held finger rubs and stops, two fingers, six fingers; reset (no click, starts again); LFOs slow and at audio rate; 24 corner and random settings with a touch at 44.1 / 48 / 96 kHz; everything open and closed again; CPU (about 25 % of one core in Node with 16 cells, 13 % with 8).
- `PW=/opt/node22/lib/node_modules/playwright SHOTS=/tmp/shots node tests/feedbacks/browser.test.js lattice` - the shared browser test of the series plus the lattice parts: starts by itself, readout names the loudest cell, the grid view draws, the pattern has changed after 12 s, REGEN lowest = all cells die away, a mouse touch in the top-left corner strikes the cell there, dragging rubs the bottom right, lifting lets go, preset Whirl + reset.
- `node tests/feedbacks/clicks.test.js lattice` - no clicks when a parameter changes while the sound runs (see `tests/feedbacks/README.md`). The parameters that set the cells against each other move only a little in this test (a real change of the pattern, not a click).

**What this does not prove:** how it sounds (whether it is a pleasure to listen to or just a field of sines that fade in and out), Safari / iOS, the CPU load on a phone. Which settings develop nicely was found by measuring (how many cells are awake, how often they change), not by listening.
