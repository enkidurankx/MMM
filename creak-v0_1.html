creak

A plate that is bowed. Up to 48 modes of a metal plate, played by friction: a bow that sticks and slips, the way a violin string or a wet finger on glass does. It squeals, creaks, rubs and sings, and the amplitude is whatever the friction allows. The bow plays by itself and looks for a place that sounds; touch the plate to bow it yourself. Silent until you start it.
