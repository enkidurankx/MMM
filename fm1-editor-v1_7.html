SYSEX DEVICE #
Notes arrive but the bank doesn't? Try the short SysEx first: if even that does not show up in a MIDI monitor, SysEx is blocked (check the permission prompt and "SysEx" above). Long SysEx often gets lost over Bluetooth MIDI — connect the FM-1 with its USB-C cable, or save the .syx and send it with a tool that paces the transfer (e.g. SysEx Librarian on macOS).

LIVE (top bar): while on, every change in the editor — randomize, mutate, init, paste, patch change, knob edits (0.4 s after the last one) — is sent to the FM-1 as Program Change + the whole voice. It overwrites the FM-1 preset with that number (FM-1+VA firmware; with the stock firmware nothing arrives).

PERFORMANCEFM-1+VA firmware · CC on the note channel · values 0-127

    

      

    
Needs the alternative FM-1+VA firmware (baudgirl.com). Send voice overwrites the preset selected on the FM-1 immediately and without a question — choose one you can lose. With LIVE on (top bar) every edit is mirrored to the FM-1 as Program Change + voice and keeps overwriting that preset (write wear of the FM-1 memory is unknown — switch LIVE off when you are done). The knobs below and EFFECTS always send when you touch them. Set the note channel above to match the FM-1 (factory: All).
