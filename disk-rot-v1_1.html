How it works: This simulator models the actual signal chain of a failing CD player. Bit errors are injected at the 16-bit PCM level (weighted toward LSB for subtle noise, MSB for spikes). When the CIRC error correction is overwhelmed, the system chooses between interpolation (bandwidth-limited neighbor averaging) and muting (32-sample cosine fade to silence). Skips and stutters are read from a 2-second ring buffer. All parameters scale with Rot Severity as a master multiplier.
