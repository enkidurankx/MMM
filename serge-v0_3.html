serge

A wave multiplier in a resonant loop, played like an instrument. Four voices; each is a loop of resonators and a wave folder after the Serge patch. A struck key makes a burst of noise; held, the loop sings by itself, and the harder it is pushed the more the folder turns level into overtones. Metal, glass, bells, pipes. Silent until you play a key.
