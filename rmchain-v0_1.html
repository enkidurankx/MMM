rm.chain

Up to sixteen ring modulators in a row. They share one carrier and one dry/wet; every tone f turns into f + fc and f − fc, an even number of stages brings the original back. An LFO moves the carrier for all of them at once, last of all as sample and hold. Silent until you start it.
