DENKI 電気

An instrument for the sounds of FuMi-1: 21 voices on a 6-operator FM engine (koto, harp, chimes, rin, shakuhachi, flutes, sho, kokyu, strings, taiko, wood blocks), played from a chromatic keyboard with polyphonic aftertouch.

SET 二: 21 more voices of DENKI's own on the same engine (shamisen, biwa, gekkin, glass koto, rain, neon koto, temple bell, wind chime, kane, mokkin, hichiriki, horagai, nokan, neon lead, mist and night pads, glass EP, neon bass, kotsuzumi, otsuzumi, dora gong). Switch sets at the top of VOICE.

SET 三: 21 electronic voices after the same instruments (circuit koto, chip shamisen, saw biwa, laser harp, arp tick, signal, digi rin, crystal, ring bell, star chime, synth shakuhachi, square flute, sho organ, sync lead, cloud sweep, city strings, FM keys, sub wobble, synth taiko, metal hit, zap clap).

SET 四: 21 fully digital, strange voices, still after the same instruments (broken koto, quantum shamisen, bit insect, reverse koto, stutter pluck, EM field, bell vortex, silicon bell, mirror gong, rin swarm, robot voice, noise flute, hopping sho, siren lead, space drone, sand pad, liquid keys, grind bass, laser kick, crush snare, machine click).

Playing

  • Strike: the lower you hit a key, the harder the note.
  • Aftertouch (per note) plays FuMi-1's own ornaments on that note: light pressure its vibrato (VIB RATE / DEPTH), full pressure 押し手 oshide (a whole tone up) or the trill, whose strokes then follow the pressure (quiet at FULL AT, full force at the bottom: a roll that swells). On a phone, holding a key builds pressure by itself (AUTO AT, AUTO time); sliding up the key adds to it. A pen or a pressure-sensing screen gives real pressure.
  • Bend (per note): slide sideways, up to two semitones.
  • MIDI: poly aftertouch, channel pressure and MPE (pitch bend and pressure per channel) all work. In 和 the MIDI keyboard plays the ST-50 rows too: white keys step through the scale (middle C = the first key), black keys the upper row.
  • Keys: 和 ST-50 (default) as on FuMi-1: the white keys are the lower row (mi fa la ti do, three times, and a top mi), the black keys the upper row; HON (本数) sets the key, SCALE the scale (in, yo, min'yo), TUNING equal, the measured Suiko 純正律, koto or user. Tonics are lit. CHROMATIC is an ordinary piano keyboard.
  • Computer keys: 和: Q–I and A–K white, 1–0 and - black; chromatic: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P. Z / X octave, Shift = full pressure on the held notes.
  • Per voice (on by default): a new voice brings its own pressure settings, after how it is played: koto 押し手, bells and flutes a trill, shakuhachi a deep slow vibrato, percussion a roll, harp, sho and strings vibrato only. Change them freely; the next voice brings its own again.
  • Hands: the koto's left hand and the other techniques of FuMi-1, held while you press the pad.
