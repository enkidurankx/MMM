entropy

A signal that is destroyed in a circle. What you hear goes through a real audio codec (Opus, at a very low bitrate), the damage comes back and is folded, modulated by an analog oscillator and sent through the codec again. Packets are lost, bits are flipped, the bitrate wanders. Nothing is a sample or a note. Silent until you start it.
