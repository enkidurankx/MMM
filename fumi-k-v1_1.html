FuMi·K

The FM engine and the screen of FuMi-1, a firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 inspired by the Suiko ST-50, played from an ordinary chromatic keyboard. What you hear is the FuMi-1 firmware itself, running as WebAssembly at 44.1 kHz.

Playing

  • Voice: Master is the volume, Key (本数) the key, Voice the instrument (21: koto, chimes, flutes, sho, kokyu, taiko ...), Scale the scale of the original keys.
  • Page knobs 1–4 change the four values on the screen. FX, ENV, EDIT, HOME switch the page.
  • LFO vibrato, ARP trill, GLO mono / poly, SEL tuning. Hold a button to reach its second page; Shift-click (or right-click) latches it.
  • The keyboard plays equal-tempered notes straight into the engine. Computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P, Z / X octave.
  • The looper (REC, PLAY) and the black-key ornaments belong to the FM-1's own key rows; with this keyboard they may not take part.
