lichen

Spectral growth. A row of up to 128 partials, each one a cell of a living field: where the field grows, the partial sounds; it spreads to its neighbours, splits, starves and dies. Left alone such a field freezes or dies, so it is kept alive: seeds fall into it, its rules wander, and a regulator holds it at the edge between frozen and dead. Touch the field to plant a seed. Silent until you start it.
